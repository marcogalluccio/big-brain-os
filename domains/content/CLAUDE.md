This domain covers professional content you produce for your own brand: posts, articles, talks, newsletters, videos, and other public-facing work. For background on your content strategy and channels, see `Context.md` in this folder.

## What This Domain Covers

- Content strategy: channels, formats, cadence, audience
- Drafts and published pieces: posts, articles, talk scripts, newsletters
- Talks and speaking engagements: proposals, slide decks, speaker bios
- Content performance notes and what to do differently next time

Not covered here: content produced on behalf of a client (use `clients-projects`), or skills you are building to produce better content (use `learning-skills`).

## Key Files

- `Context.md`: background, channels, goals, audience, reference links
- Add subfolders per channel or format as needed (e.g. `linkedin/`, `talks/`, `newsletter/`)

## When to Read This File

Read this file when:
- Drafting or editing any post, article, or talk for your brand
- Planning a content calendar or reviewing what to publish next
- Preparing for a speaking engagement or media appearance

## Domain-Specific Rules

- Present copy variations as options in chat; apply only after you choose one
- No em-dash in any public-facing copy: use commas, colons, or periods instead
- Keep short display copy on one line (headlines, CTAs, slide titles); fix layout rather than wrapping
